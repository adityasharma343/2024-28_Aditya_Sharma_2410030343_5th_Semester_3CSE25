## 📌 About the Simulation

The simulation is a self-paced, virtual programme in which participants work through practical tasks modelled on the day-to-day work of a corporate cybersecurity team. My tasks covered both halves of a phishing-awareness exercise: **designing a phishing email simulation**, and **interpreting phishing simulation results** to decide what an organisation should do next.

**Organisations:** [Mastercard](https://www.mastercard.com/) is a global payments technology company. [Forage](https://www.theforage.com/) hosts free, self-paced virtual job simulations built with leading employers and issues a certificate on completion.

> **Note:** This was a virtual learning simulation. It involved no live organisational systems and no real targets, and there was no stipend associated with it.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Understand how phishing and social-engineering attacks work and which indicators make an email convincing or suspicious
* Design a phishing email simulation to test and improve employee security awareness
* Interpret phishing simulation results and identify what they reveal about user behaviour
* Turn findings into practical recommendations, such as training and reporting guidance
* Complete both practical tasks and earn the Certificate of Completion
